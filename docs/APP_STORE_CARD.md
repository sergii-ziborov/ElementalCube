Альфа 0.1.0 (2) — первая прогулка Льдинки.

• Удерживайте стрелки, чтобы катиться или плыть.

### Описание App Store

```text
Small cube. Big world.

Meet a curious fluffy ice cube on its first walk along a quiet shore. Help it reach the flag, collect crystals, and discover how the world responds to each move.

Drag and release to jump along your chosen arc. Roll over grass, push a wooden crate, slide on ice, and swim back to shore. Frost Breath turns the water surface into a temporary path. Cross before it melts.

Your cube has a personality: it focuses before a jump, celebrates discoveries, frowns in the water, and shakes its wet fur. Soft grass and hard stone create different landings. Supported iPhones add tactile feedback to the experience.

This is an early ElementalCube alpha with one small map for testing controls, physics, and the cube's character. Training, evolution, and arenas are still in development.

No account, ads, or game server connection is required. Designed for iPhone and iPad in portrait and landscape.

The current in-game interface is in Russian.
```


### Описание TestFlight

Welcome to the first ElementalCube alpha!

Meet a fluffy ice cube with a personality. Roll, jump along an aimed arc, push a crate, swim, and freeze the water on a quiet shore. Reach the flag and try to collect all three crystals.

We are testing how the controls feel and how clearly the world reacts. This build includes one map, wet fur, facial expressions, sliding, and different landing responses. Control and physics settings persist between launches. iPhone haptics can be turned off in Settings.

### Что проверить — TestFlight

```text
Alpha 0.1.0 (2): the cube's first walk.

• Hold the arrows to roll or swim.
• Pull the jump button back and release; the dotted arc previews your jump. A quick tap jumps upward.
• Leave the water without ice by swimming toward the bank or using the jump button for a water stroke.
• Frost Breath costs 30 energy and creates ice for 25 seconds. Casting while immersed leaves an opening around the cube.
• Compare grass and stone landings, sliding on ice, and iPhone haptics.
• Change aiming direction and camera zoom, restart, and check that settings persist.
• Rotate your device and return from the background. Controls should not remain pressed.

Please focus on button comfort, predictable jumps, getting out of water, ice visibility, and fur reactions. Include your device model and where the issue occurred in feedback.
```
